HTTrack 3.50 beta 1, the first Android build in nine years.

• HTTPS works again: OpenSSL 3, TLS 1.3, SOCKS5 proxies
• Files over 2 GB and accented names no longer break a mirror
• A failed update no longer overwrites your good copy
• Captures srcset, lazy-loaded images, video and audio sources
• New: sitemap seeding, single-file pages, WARC output
• 64-bit, dark mode, right-to-left layouts, Android 16

Beta: please report problems on GitHub.
